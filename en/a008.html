Product description

Regular car wash soap foam won't cling. It breaks down after a couple of rinses, and your wash mitt ends up dragging grit across the paint. Foam Car Wash goes high-concentrate instead: one bottle makes about 50 liters of working solution, with rich, fine foam that lifts and encapsulates grit to cut down on scratch risk while you wipe.

How to use it

  1. Rinse the grit and larger debris off the body first with water or a pre-wash.
  2. Dilute per the label, then use a foam cannon or sprayer to cover the whole car evenly with foam.
  3. Let the foam sit for a bit to soften the dirt, then wipe with a wash mitt from the roof down in straight, single-direction strokes. Don't go in circles.
  4. Rinse clean top to bottom, section by section, then dry off.

Things to know

  • Don't wash the car in direct sun or when the body is hot to the touch. The foam will dry too fast and leave marks on the paint.
  • Keep your wash mitt and towels clean. Grit trapped in the fabric is the main cause of scratches.
  • For this product's pH and detailed specs, go by the label, or message us on WhatsApp to confirm with our team before use. Don't mix it at another product's ratio.
  • Wear gloves and keep the area ventilated. If it gets in your eyes, flush with plenty of water and see a doctor.
  • Store in a cool, dark place away from heat and direct sunlight. Keep out of reach of children. Do not swallow.
