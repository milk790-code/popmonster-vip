Our five commitments

How we
run our membership.

How POP MONSTER runs a long-term business — not a slogan, but a design principle.

01

Product value first

Every part of our membership program is built on real car-care products that work well, not on making money from recruiting people or on virtual goods to prop it up.

02

Compliant and transparent

Designed to follow Taiwan's Fair Trade Act, Consumer Protection Act, Personal Data Protection Act, and merchandise voucher regulations, with a written legal review before official launch.

03

Financially predictable

No permanent payouts, no unlimited replacements, no lifetime promises. Every benefit has a time limit, conditions, and a cap.

04

Users have a way out

Stored value comes with a performance guarantee, subscriptions have a 7-day cooling-off period, personal data can be deleted anytime, and members can cancel anytime with no penalty.

05

Built for the long run

We're designing a membership program meant to last 30 years, not a fast-growth model that collapses in 30 months. Speed isn't the goal. Stability is.

Launch status

2. Set up a stored-value performance guarantee with a partner bank

3. Write and publish the standard membership contract and privacy policy

4. Finish building the system and run a limited pilot

5. Have the lawyer review the pilot, then announce the official launch
