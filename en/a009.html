Product description

White cars showing dull white blotches, yellow stains on the paint, bird-dropping marks and acid-rain marks that won't wash off no matter what — these aren't ordinary dirt, they're stains that have already etched into the paint. Liquid Eraser has no abrasives in it. Apply it directly and wipe to treat bird droppings, acid-rain marks, tree sap, and spots, with more noticeable results on light-colored paint (white, yellow, silver, light gray). You can wipe it on by hand or use it with a dual-action polisher and an ultra-soft foam pad. It treats stains, not scratches.

How to use it

  1. Shake the product well before use.
  2. Wash the car clean and park it in a shady, dust-free spot before you start.
  3. By hand: apply some to a sponge or towel and work it over the stained area in small sections, back and forth.
  4. Wipe clean and buff with a clean towel afterward.
  5. To speed things up, use a dual-action polisher with an ultra-soft foam pad instead.

Things to know

  • Always wash the car clean before applying. Wiping over dust or grit still on the body is like sanding the paint with sandpaper.
  • Work in a shady, dust-free spot. Don't apply it in direct sun or on a hot body.
  • This product has no abrasives. It's for removing dirt and stains, not a scratch repair product, so don't use it on scratches.
  • For cars with a coating or wrap already applied: this product's pH and ingredient data haven't been finalized for release, and we have no test data on coating compatibility. Message us on WhatsApp first, or test on a small hidden area first.
  • The usual precautions for cleaning products apply here too: keep the area ventilated while you work, wear gloves to avoid prolonged skin contact, and if it gets in your eyes, flush immediately with plenty of water.
  • Keep out of reach of children and pets. Do not ingest.
