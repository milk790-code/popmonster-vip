Product description

If your car gets dusty again just a few days after washing, or rain leaves water spots that won't come off — Angel Coating Guard is a paint maintenance spray you use after washing. Right after a contact wash, while the car is still wet, dilute it and spray the whole car, go over it lightly with a clean wash mitt, then rinse and dry it off. Works on paint, PPF and color-change film, glass, and plastic trim. If the windshield already has a coating and the surface is clean, spraying it directly won't conflict, and it can even help reduce wiper noise.

Specs

Compatible surfaces
Paint, PPF and color-change film, glass, plastic trim; TPU, PVC, and other film surfaces
Not for use on
Windshields that haven't been deep cleaned (a contaminated windshield will turn greasy, hazy, or cloudy)
Dilution ratio
Follow the ratio on the bottle. A pump mist sprayer and a PA foam lance need different dilution ratios, so message us on WhatsApp with which sprayer you're using and we'll give you the right ratio.

How to use it

  1. Wash the car first, then remove any oil film and degrease. This product soaks into paint, color-change film, and PPF, so if the prep isn't clean it will cause problems.
  2. Right after a normal wash, leave the car wet — no need to dry it first.
  3. Dilute it per the bottle's instructions, then spray the whole car evenly with a sprayer — don't miss any spots.
  4. Go over it lightly once with a clean wash mitt — don't scrub hard, and don't buff it.
  5. Rinse and dry it off. Done.

Things to know

  • Always wash, strip the oil film, and degrease before applying. Skipping deep cleaning first will cause problems.
  • Don't spray it on a windshield that hasn't been deep cleaned — it will turn greasy, hazy, or cloudy.
  • If the windshield already has a glass coating and the surface is clean, you can spray it directly — it won't conflict, and it can even help reduce wiper noise.
  • This product can be applied in direct sun. But other products from the same brand (like Papaya Monster Wash Base) must never be applied in high heat or direct sun. The rules are opposite, so check which product you have in hand before you start.
  • The dilution ratio varies a lot depending on the sprayer (a pump mist sprayer and a PA foam lance are different) — don't just copy someone else's ratio, check which one you're using first.
  • To remove it: mist on this brand's heavy-duty leather cleaner undiluted, or pre-wash with an anionic surfactant. It comes off after several washes.
  • We don't currently have a full ingredient list, pH value, or Safety Data Sheet (SDS) available to share. If you have allergies or concerns about the ingredients, ask us before you buy.
  • General precautions for chemical cleaning products: wear gloves and work in a ventilated area, and avoid contact with eyes and skin. If it gets in your eyes, flush immediately with plenty of water and see a doctor.
