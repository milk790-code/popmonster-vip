THREE EVIDENCE CHAINS

It's not another back end. It's a clear, easy-to-follow record of every service.

01 / BEFORE SERVICE

Make it clear to the customer first

Photos, plain-language explanations, time, and price are laid out clearly before booking.

02 / DURING SERVICE

Keep a record of vehicle condition and progress

Booking, deposit, vehicle condition, the job, and sign-off all follow the same flow.

03 / AFTER SERVICE

Give the next visit something to go on

Members can see their balance, bookings, and history. Shops keep a traceable ledger.
