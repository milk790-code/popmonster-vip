Product description

Glass looks hazy, wiping leaves a film of water spots, and fingerprints and oil film won't come off no matter how you wipe—this is the Glass Cleaner, a daily glass care spray. Neutral formula, use it straight with no dilution: spray it on, wipe, and it dries with no streaks. Besides car glass, it also works on windows at home, bathroom mirrors, glass doors, display cases, and glass tabletops.

How to use it

  1. Rinse or dust off any visible dirt and grit on the glass first, so wiping doesn't scratch it.
  2. Spray it directly on the glass, or spray it onto a clean microfiber cloth first.
  3. Wipe in one direction, then use the clean side of the cloth to dry it off before it's fully dry on its own.
  4. On large areas, work section by section. Finish one section before moving to the next to avoid streaks.

Things to know

  • This is a daily care spray for cleaning glass, not a glass coating or ceramic-type coating. It won't leave a water-beading layer on the glass.
  • If the glass already has stubborn oil film or mineral deposits, treat it first with a dedicated oil-film remover. This one is for daily maintenance after that.
  • Don't apply it in direct sun when the glass is hot to the touch. The product will dry too fast and leave streaks.
  • Avoid spraying it on interior electronic panels, the dashboard, or leather. If it gets on them, wipe it off right away with water.
  • Wear gloves and keep the area ventilated. If it gets in your eyes, flush with plenty of water and see a doctor.
  • Store in a cool, dark place away from heat and direct sunlight. Keep out of reach of children. Do not swallow.
