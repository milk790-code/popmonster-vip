Product description

Leather seats turning yellow, dry, and stiff. White interiors or color-sensitive leather you're afraid to clean in case it fades or leaves marks. Gentle Leather Cleaner (Mousse) uses a gentle mild-acid formula. Use it undiluted. It comes with a mousse bottle: pump the foam onto a towel and wipe gently along the grain. No need to wipe off residue separately or rinse. It handles light to medium soiling; not for aniline leather, semi-aniline leather, suede, or nubuck.

How to use it

  1. Pour the concentrate from the large bottle into the mousse bottle and refill as needed. Keep the large bottle sealed and out of light.
  2. Pump foam from the mousse bottle onto a towel. Don't spray it directly onto the leather.
  3. Wipe gently along the leather grain to clean light to medium soiling.
  4. No need to wipe off residue separately, and no rinsing needed.
  5. After cleaning, always follow up with a leather conditioner or oil-replenishing product.
  6. Shops and studios can pair it with their own tools as needed.

Things to know

  • Not for these four leather types: aniline, semi-aniline, suede, and nubuck.
  • Don't wipe it on dry — always foam it up in the mousse bottle and put the foam on a towel first.
  • Don't use it on plastic trim; rubber parts are fine.
  • Keep the large bottle of concentrate sealed and out of light.
  • This product only handles light to medium soiling. For heavy grease or stubborn stains that a mild acid can't break down, use a heavy-duty leather cleaner instead — don't force the mild acid to do that job.
  • For color-sensitive leather or white/light-colored interiors, test on a small hidden area first to make sure it doesn't fade or leave marks before doing the whole surface.
  • Wear gloves and keep the car ventilated while working, and avoid eye contact. If it gets in your eyes, flush with plenty of water.
  • Keep out of reach of children and pets.
