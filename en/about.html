Chapter 05 · Service Policy

Service
promise.

Know this before you buy — every line here is true.

Shipping timeline
In Taiwan, Shopee orders ship on the platform's own timeline. For overseas orders, we first confirm on WhatsApp whether each item can ship to your country and what shipping costs, then you pay. For special bulk orders or stock shortages, we'll message you on WhatsApp.
Response time
We reply within 4 hours during business hours on the LINE official account, and within 24 hours outside business hours. We're busy with overseas orders right now, so replies may take a little longer, but we never miss a message.
Returns & exchanges
Shopee orders in Taiwan follow Shopee's platform return policy. For overseas orders or product defects, keep photos as proof and message us on WhatsApp for help.
Overseas shipping
We handle international orders. For overseas shipping, message us on WhatsApp directly to work out delivery.
Wholesale / contracts
For wholesale, samples, or contract partnerships, message us on WhatsApp with your shop type and needs.
Safety notice
All our chemicals are for car-care use only. Read the instructions before use. Do not swallow and keep out of eyes. We recommend wearing gloves during application.
