About

POP MONSTER × MISO — built for pros

Origin

POP MONSTER was born on the floor of Taiwanese auto-detailing shops. Our founders spent years watching detailers struggle with harsh chemistry, inconsistent results, steep learning curves, and easy-to-make mistakes for newcomers. We believe great detailing products shouldn't be reserved for veterans — anyone entering the trade should be able to do the job safely, easily, and with pride.

Three Principles

✦ Safety always first — neutral chemistry, minimal occupational risk
✦ Easy for everyone — standardized dilution and steps, beginner-proof
✦ Zero-risk in use — safe on paint, films, and coatings

Five Series

  • Coating — Angel Coating, Glass Coating, Crystal Sealant for long-lasting protection
  • Compound — MISO 80/600/1000 full polishing gradient
  • Pads — RO polishing pads for pros
  • Cleaning — Iron, wheel, engine bay scenario-based cleaning
  • Care — Leather, plastic, tire, interior & exterior care

Partners

MISO is our compound partner. All products ship via Shopee Taiwan (milk790), with LINE / WhatsApp pre- and post-sales support.
