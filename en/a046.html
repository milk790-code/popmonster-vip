Product description

Still not slick after a wash, water spots and mineral rings that won't come off, a coated car losing its self-cleaning finish, or degreasing before and after polishing — this is an acidic, functional contact-wash foam with a concentrate pH of about 4.0. What sets it apart from a regular car wash soap is that the ratio decides the function: the same bottle mixed strong strips residue and water spots, mixed weak brings the coating's self-cleaning finish back. So decide which effect you want before you start.

How to use it

  1. First decide what you're doing this time: removing water spots, restoring the paint's self-cleaning finish, or degreasing before and after polishing. With this product, the ratio decides the effect, not just the strength.
  2. With a pressure washer PA foam lance: 1:5 for light water spots, QD residue, traffic film, and car wax; 1:10 to restore the self-cleaning finish on paint with a ceramic-type coating without harming long-lasting coatings; 1:15 to restore the self-cleaning finish on quick coatings and wax.
  3. Switching to a pump foam sprayer, electric sprayer, or foam bucket, use 10 times the PA ratio — that's 1:50 to 1:150.
  4. When diluting, add water first, then the product, and shake well.
  5. Spray evenly over the body, out of direct light. Don't apply in strong sun or when the body is hot.
  6. Wipe gently with a clean wash mitt.
  7. Rinse completely with a pressure washer — don't let it dry on the paint.

Things to know

  • Do not let it stay in extended contact with alloy wheels, chrome-plated parts, unsealed bare metal, or rubber trim.
  • Never mix with alkaline cleaners or bleach-based products — this can cause a dangerous reaction.
  • Contains 1,2-benzisothiazolin-3(2H)-one, which may cause an allergic reaction.
  • Wear gloves when handling.
  • For surfaces of unknown material, test first before use. If you're unsure about a specific area's material, test on a hidden spot first.
  • Do not apply in hot conditions, or when the surface you're working on is already hot.
  • If the car has a coating or ceramic-type coating, confirm which type before you start: a 1:3 to 1:5 ratio will fully strip a quick coating, and can also strip a long-lasting coating that didn't bond well. A PA ratio of 1:10 can still cause a quick coating to degrade.
  • Rinse completely — don't let the product dry on the paint.
  • If it gets on skin or in eyes, rinse right away with running water and see a doctor if needed.
  • Keep out of reach of children. Do not ingest.
  • Store sealed in a cool, ventilated place.
