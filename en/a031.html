Product description

Afraid to touch white seats yourself, worried light-colored fabric will fade, dashboard and door panel buttons feel greasy — interior cleaning usually means keeping several dedicated products on hand. The All-Purpose Cleaner takes the one-bottle route: load it into a foam sprayer, spray it on the dirt, and wipe with a soft towel until the dirt is gone. No extra rinsing, no worrying about residue. White and light-colored fabric, interior leather, plastic buttons and dashboards, and interior metal trim are all covered.

Specs

Capacity
200ml / 500ml (retail bottle)
Dilution ratio
Retail bottle is ready to use; concentrate version 1:5
Compatible surfaces
White/light-colored fabric (car seats, interior carpet, seat covers), interior leather (seats, steering wheel, door panel leather), plastic parts (interior buttons, dashboard, door panels), metal parts (interior metal trim, body metal trim)

How to use it

  1. Use with a foam sprayer and a soft towel.
  2. Load into a foam sprayer and spray evenly on the dirt.
  3. Wipe with a soft towel until the dirt is gone.
  4. No extra rinsing needed, and no worry about residue.

Things to know

  • Only use on the materials listed in the spec table. We don't have compatibility data yet for reactive metals like anodized aluminum or chrome-plated parts, or for paint coatings and color-change wraps, so don't apply it to those directly.
  • On leather, light-colored fabric, and aged interior parts, test on a small hidden area first. Confirm there's no change in color or feel before treating the whole surface.
  • Wear gloves and keep the area ventilated while applying, especially if you're prone to skin irritation.
  • Don't mix with other cleaners.
  • If it gets in your eyes or on your skin, flush immediately with plenty of water. Keep out of reach of children and pets to avoid accidental ingestion.
  • Store away from high heat and direct sunlight. Don't leave it in the car long-term.
