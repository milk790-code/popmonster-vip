Product description

You peel off a sticker or pull down a dash cam mount and you're left with a sticky blob of adhesive. A scraper risks scratches, and a strong solvent can leave a smell that lingers. This is a water-based adhesive remover with no harsh solvents. Spray it on, let it sit a few minutes so it breaks down the adhesive, then wipe it off with a clean microfiber towel. It handles double-sided tape, label adhesive, sticker residue, tree sap, and bug residue. Adhesive left behind after peeling off wrap film is its specialty.

Most surfaces with adhesive residue: car paint, plastic trim, paint protection film (PPF), color-change wrap, light covers, glass, metal (test a small hidden area first if you're not sure about the material)

How to use it

  1. Before you start, clear dust and debris off the surface and make sure it's dry, with no visible water spots.
  2. Spray this product evenly over the adhesive, making sure the adhesive layer is fully covered, and let it sit for 3-5 minutes so the formula can penetrate and break it down.
  3. For stubborn, thick adhesive, spray a second time based on how well it's breaking down, and extend the sit time to 5-10 minutes.
  4. Once the adhesive has fully softened and broken down, wipe gently with a clean microfiber towel until all residue is gone.
  5. For large areas, work section by section so the product doesn't evaporate before it can break down the adhesive.

Things to know

  • Store in a cool, dry, ventilated place, out of direct sunlight and high heat, and keep out of reach of children and pets.
  • Even though this is a mild water-based formula, we recommend wearing protective gloves during use to avoid repeated, prolonged skin contact.
  • If it splashes into your eyes, flush immediately with plenty of running water for at least 15 minutes and get medical attention right away.
  • Do not spray on food-contact surfaces or eating utensils. Wash your hands thoroughly after use.
  • This is a topical cleaning product, not for ingestion. If accidentally swallowed, bring the packaging with you and get medical attention right away.
  • Do not mix with other chemical cleaners, as this can cause a reaction that produces harmful substances.
  • The list above says "most" surfaces with adhesive residue because we don't have full material compatibility test data. If you're not sure about a material, test a small hidden area first.
  • We don't provide an ingredient list or Safety Data Sheet (SDS) for this product, and the pH isn't listed. Keep the area ventilated while you work.

FAQ

It works on most wrap-film adhesive residue (acrylic-based glue). Special industrial double-sided tape may need a longer sit time or a repeat application. Not recommended for weatherstrip adhesive.

This formula is safe on factory paint. Use a controlled amount rather than soaking the surface, and rinse once with clean water when you're done.

After removing the adhesive, we recommend using Prep Degreaser Spray to clear any leftover solvent and get the surface clean again, then apply Angel Coating Guard for protection.
