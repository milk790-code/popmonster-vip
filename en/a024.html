Product description

Yellowed tires, faded white bumpers and wheel arches, hazy door handle trim - this is the protectant for tires and black plastic trim on the body. It's made with antioxidants, solvents, penetrants, and natural peppermint oil, and you use it straight, no dilution. Apply it to a tire surface that's fully cleaned and dry, plus side skirts, body kits, wheel arches, the plastic parts of the front and rear bumpers, door handle trim, and black plastic in the engine bay. Works on cars and motorcycles.

Things to know

  • Make sure the surface is fully dry before you apply it. Applying it to a wet surface gives uneven results.
  • Apply a thin coat. Don't build up a thick layer.
  • Avoid contact with your mouth, nose, and eyes. If it gets in them, rinse immediately with water.
  • Contains natural peppermint oil, which is a common allergen. If you have sensitive skin, wear gloves and work in a ventilated area.
  • Store sealed in a cool, dry place, away from heat, open flame, and direct sunlight, out of children's reach.
  • We don't have tested pH data or a safety data sheet for this product yet, so we can't make any claims about its acidity/alkalinity or toxicity.
  • We don't have data yet on materials to avoid or compatibility with other products, so don't mix it with other chemicals. If you're not sure about a material, test it on a small hidden area first.
