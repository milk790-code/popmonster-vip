Product description

Wheels that never come clean, a black film of brake dust, calipers that stay gray no matter how much you scrub. This cleaner targets heavy grease and brake dust, with an iron-removal booster, surfactants, and deionized water. Besides wheels, tires, and the running gear, it also works on the engine bay, bug residue and bird droppings on the body, and grease left on paint. The dilution ratio can vary up to tenfold depending on your application tool, so follow the label, or message us on WhatsApp before you dilute it.

How to use it

  1. First decide which tool you'll use to apply it (pressure washer, mist sprayer, or foam cannon). The dilution ratio can vary up to tenfold between tools, so follow the label or ask us for the ratio.
  2. Once diluted, spray it evenly over the target area. Avoid hot brake rotors and hot engine metal parts.
  3. Dwell time: 3 to 5 minutes for wheels, tires, and the running gear; 5 to 8 minutes for the engine bay; 3 to 5 minutes for bug residue and bird droppings on the body; 2 to 3 minutes for degreasing paint.
  4. Use a soft brush to gently agitate heavy grime and gaps. No need to scrub hard—avoid scratching the surface.
  5. Rinse: use a pressure washer for general areas, but always use a low-pressure hose for the engine bay.
  6. Rinse it off thoroughly right after application. Don't let the product dry on the surface.

Things to know

  • This product's safe cleaning performance assumes room temperature, no direct sunlight, and a vehicle body that's still in factory condition, without aging or damage.
  • Be extremely careful with aftermarket parts, chrome-plated parts, or anodized surfaces. Test on a small hidden area first to check for any issues before using it. If you do need to use it, dilute it more.
  • For any aftermarket part where you're not sure of the material, or that shows signs of aging, always test on a small hidden area first for compatibility.
  • Never apply it in strong sun or high heat, and don't let the concentrate or diluted solution dry on the surface for a long time.
  • Don't scrub hard—avoid scratching the base surface.
  • Don't spray it on hot brake rotors or hot engine metal parts. Always rinse the engine bay with a low-pressure hose, never a direct pressure-washer blast.
  • We don't have tested pH data for this product, and we don't make any claims about its acidity or alkalinity. For surfaces sensitive to pH, test first before applying it over a large area.
  • We don't have compatibility data for mixing yet, so don't mix it with other cleaners or chemicals.
  • Wear gloves and work in a ventilated area. Avoid contact with eyes and skin. If contact occurs, flush thoroughly with water.
  • Store in a cool place, out of reach of children. Don't leave the bottle in the car.

FAQ

For chrome-plated, plated, or anodized surfaces, test on a small hidden area first. Once you confirm there's no issue, apply it to the full surface, shorten the dwell time (1 to 2 minutes), and avoid prolonged contact. If you're not sure about the material, message us on WhatsApp first.

We recommend a deep wheel clean every 1 to 2 washes, especially for vehicles that often drive mountain roads or highways. Brake dust buildup over time can corrode the wheel paint, so cleaning it early saves work later.

A brush gets dust out of tight spots in the wheel, especially on 5-spoke wheels, for a more thorough clean. But for light everyday cleaning, spraying it on, letting it dwell, then rinsing with a pressure washer works fine too.
