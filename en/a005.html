Product description

Holograms after polishing, hairline scratches covering the surface after fine polishing, a hazy mirror finish, no clarity no matter what you do — the problem is usually in that last finishing step. MISO No.10 is the 1000-grit mirror finish compound that comes after No.3 (80 grit) and No.5 (600 grit), handling the final fine polish in the compounding process. Use it undiluted, straight from the bottle. Works with RO, GA, and DA machines. Apply with a one-step mirror wool pad to cut first, then finish with a foam pad.

Pads to pair with
One-step mirror wool pad (cutting stage) + foam pad (finishing stage). This is a starting point, not the only way to do it. Adjust the pad and grit combo based on the result you're seeing as you work. Let how the paint looks after each pass decide your next step.

How to use it

  1. Pair with a one-step mirror wool pad for the cutting stage; this step can cut up to 1500 grit.
  2. Finish by switching to a foam pad and going over the whole panel again.
  3. After finishing, check every panel under a detailing light to make sure there are no visible hairline scratches before handing the car back.

Things to know

  • On cars with paint that's already aged or oxidized (like an old solid black Honda), remove the oxidized layer with a coarser grit first, then finish with this one — going straight to the No.10 won't do anything. If the oxidation has soaked all the way through and the paint still won't come back after polishing, that paint is beyond saving. It's not a compound problem.
  • Every car's paint condition is different. Test on a small, hidden area first, check the result and how the paint reacts, then do the full car.
  • This product doesn't have a pH value, ingredient list, or safety data sheet (SDS) available to the public right now. If your workplace requires these documents, message us on WhatsApp first before you buy.
  • Machine polishing generates dust and heat. Work in a ventilated area and wear gloves and eye protection.
  • If it gets in your eyes or on your skin, rinse immediately with plenty of water. See a doctor if you feel unwell.
  • Store in a cool, dark place away from heat and direct sunlight. Keep out of reach of children. Do not swallow.

FAQ

If the No.5 one-step polish already gets you a mirror finish you're happy with, you don't need the No.10. If you're after the ultimate mirror finish (dark solid-color cars, track cars), or the No.3 80-grit step left visible marks, the No.10 is a necessary finishing step.

Works best with the black mirror foam pad (A033). It's the densest, finest-surface pad, which lets the No.10's mirror-finishing effect come through fully.

After polishing, we recommend applying Angel Coating Guard or a coating right away to seal the freshly polished paint. The less time the bare paint is left exposed, the less contamination sticks to it.
