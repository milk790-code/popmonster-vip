Product description

After washing, the paint still feels rough and gritty, with little black specks you can't pick off. That's iron fallout, industrial dust, overspray and tree sap stuck in the paint, and car wash soap won't remove it. This is the heavy-grade clay bar made for paint. Glide it lightly in circles with the Clay Lubricant to lift those particles off. It's the prep step before polishing or coating. On PPF, color-change wrap and wrapped panels, use the fine-grade bar instead. Don't use the heavy-grade bar there.

How to use it

  1. Dilute the clay lubricant first, no weaker than 1:10, and spray it evenly over the paint you're cleaning. Spray the clay too.
  2. Take about 30g of clay each time, flatten it into a disc in your palm, and wipe gently in circles. Don't press down hard.
  3. Re-spray the lubricant as you go. The moment the surface dries, spray more. Never work it dry.
  4. When you're done, rinse the paint clean with water.
  5. Rinse the clay clean after use and you can reuse it. If it drops on the ground, throw it out. Don't put it back on the car.

Things to know

  • Will clay scratch the paint? Yes, if there isn't enough lubricant. The clay and the lubricant are a set, don't use the clay dry on its own.
  • "Plain water is fine" is wrong. Water's lubricating film is too thin, and once the clay picks up iron particles, it's like dragging sandpaper across the paint.
  • Never work it dry. Spray more lubricant the moment the surface dries.
  • Don't dilute the lubricant weaker than 1:10. Go weaker and you have no lubrication at all.
  • Once the clay hits the ground, it can't go back on the car. Picking it up and carrying on just presses grit from the ground into the paint.
  • The heavy-grade and fine-grade bars aren't interchangeable: the heavy-grade bar is for paint only. Always use the fine-grade bar on PPF, color-change wrap and wrapped panels.
  • We recommend avoiding direct sun and the hottest part of the day when you work. The lubricant dries fast, and you can end up working the clay dry before you get another spray on.
  • We don't have test data we can cite on whether you need to polish afterward. If you need that, message us on WhatsApp first, or test it on a small hidden area first.
  • This product doesn't come with an ingredient list or a safety data sheet (SDS). We recommend wearing gloves and working in a ventilated area.
  • Store in a cool, dry place, out of direct sunlight and heat, and out of reach of children and pets.
