Leather Care (Leather Cleaner)

Heavy-duty leather cleaner, not a conditioner. Removes grease, stickiness, and footprints from real leather and faux leather. Not for nubuck or tech fabric.

Product description

Grease on the seats, a sticky steering wheel, a footprint on the leather seat that won't wipe off — this is usually grease, silicone oil, or old wax buildup sitting on the leather. This is a heavy-duty leather cleaner, not a conditioner: dilute anywhere from full strength to 1:10 depending on how dirty it is, then spray and wipe on real and faux leather interiors. It also works on grease on color-change wrap and PPF. Always follow up with an oil-replenishing treatment after cleaning. Not for nubuck or tech fabric.

Specs

Dilution ratio
Stubborn heavy dirt: full strength. Light grease: 1:3 to 1:5. General cleaning: 1:5 to 1:10. Don't dilute past 1:10.
Compatible surfaces
Car leather interior (seats, steering wheel, armrest, door panels, dash, etc.), faux leather interior (imitation leather seats, leather trim, etc.), plastic and rubber parts, paint, color-change wrap, PPF; household leather items (leather sofas, bags, shoes, belts, etc.)
Not for use on
Nubuck, tech fabric; leather that's already aged or been refinished.
Pads to pair with
Mist sprayer or foam bottle, soft brush, microfiber towel; after a heavy clean, follow up with a leather conditioner or other oil-replenishing product.

How to use it

  1. Get a mist sprayer or foam bottle, a soft brush, and a clean microfiber towel ready.
  2. Dilute based on how dirty it is: full strength for stubborn heavy dirt, 1:3 to 1:5 for light grease, 1:5 to 1:10 for general cleaning.
  3. Spray and wipe: spray evenly over the dirty area, then wipe gently with a clean towel, following the grain.
  4. Deep clean: for stubborn spots, scrub gently with a soft brush, then wipe with a towel lightly dampened with water to remove the residue.
  5. After a heavy clean, always follow up with a conditioner or oil-replenishing product to restore the leather's oils and keep it soft and glossy.

Things to know

  • Wear protective gloves during application to avoid prolonged skin contact. Take extra care if you have sensitive skin.
  • If it splashes into your eyes, flush immediately with plenty of water and see a doctor if you feel discomfort.
  • Never spray on food-contact surfaces. Wash your hands thoroughly after use.
  • This product is for external cleaning use only. Never ingest it. If swallowed, see a doctor immediately and bring the product with you.
  • Do not mix with other strong alkaline cleaning chemicals, to avoid a chemical reaction.
  • Not for nubuck or tech fabric; the surface must not be aged or previously refinished.
  • This is a cleaner, not a conditioner. Always follow a heavy clean with an oil-replenishing treatment — don't just wash it and walk away.
  • Store in a cool, dry, ventilated place, away from direct sunlight and heat, and out of reach of children and pets.
  • A042 Gentle Leather Cleaner (Mousse) is also a leather cleaner, but its material restrictions are different from this one (it's not for aniline leather, semi-aniline leather, suede, or nubuck). Don't apply one product's cautions to the other.
