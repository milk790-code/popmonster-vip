RO Custom Mirror Foam Pad (Black)

Product description

You finish polishing, and in the sun there are still circles of swirl marks. Outdoors, a black car shows holograms across whole panels. The marks are still visible right before you hand the car back. The problem is usually the last step: no dedicated finishing pad. The RO Custom Mirror Foam Pad (Black) is a high-density foam pad made for RO polishers. The pad face is foam, and the backing is heat-stable EVA with an added custom cushioning layer. In the standard polishing sequence, it comes after the coarse and medium foam pads as the final finishing step, used with MISO No.10 Mirror Finish (1000 grit) compound.

Things to know

  • This is a finishing pad for the final step, not a heavy-cut pad. To correct deeper marks, use the matching heavy-cut foam pad or a wool pad instead.
  • Soft black paint has its own dedicated pad. This one isn't designed for soft black paint.
  • Pad diameter, hardness grade, backing adhesive, pack quantity, and recommended safe RPM aren't published yet. For RPM, stay within your polisher's factory manual limit — if you're not sure, message us on WhatsApp first.
  • Materials to avoid and care/cleaning instructions aren't published yet either. Test on a small hidden area the first time you use it.
  • Heavy cutting with an RO polisher can burn through the paint or cause the pad to skip. Don't press down too hard.
  • Extended heavy cutting heats up the backing plate. Let the machine and pad rest partway through to avoid warping the backing plate.
  • Wear eye protection and a mask while polishing to avoid flying compound dust.
