Product description

Drive home on the highway and you get a row of black spots on the rear and bumper. Park under a tree overnight and the sap turns sticky and won't wipe off. Car wash soap won't take off tar and sap like this, and scrubbing hard just scratches the paint. With tar remover, you apply it, let it break the grime down on its own, then wipe the residue away with a soft cloth. It still works on a wet car, so you don't need to wait for the car to dry — you can do this step right in the middle of your wash.

How to use it

  1. Apply the product directly onto the tar or sap spots.
  2. Let it sit for about 2 minutes to soften and break down the residue.
  3. Wipe with a soft cloth and check the residue is completely gone.
  4. It still works on a wet car, so you don't have to wait for the car to dry first.

Things to know

  • Test on a small hidden area first, and only apply to the whole panel once you've confirmed it's fine.
  • For modified parts, chrome-plated parts, matte paint, PPF, or any surface you're not sure about, ask us on WhatsApp before you use it.
  • Wear gloves and keep the area ventilated. Avoid extended direct contact with skin.
  • If it gets on skin or in eyes, rinse immediately with plenty of water and see a doctor if needed.
  • Do not mix with other cleaners or bleach-based products.
  • Don't let the product sit on the paint for a long time or dry on its own.
  • Store sealed in a cool place, out of direct sun, and keep out of reach of children. Do not swallow.
